COMPANION — START HERE

1. Double-click Start Companion.cmd in the Companion folder.
   If you are in the parent outputs folder, Open Companion.cmd opens the same app.

2. The first time, choose your reading-group name, your name, and a password.
   Use at least 12 characters for the password.

3. Select a passage in the sample paper and click Ask a question.
   Add an explanation, review a contribution, or mark what helped you understand.

NEXT TIME
Double-click the same launcher. Sign in if asked.
Closing the browser keeps your saved questions and discussions.

This PC is already configured. No installation or terminal commands are needed.
On a fresh checkout, install Node.js 24.15 or newer once, then use the same launcher.
The shared reading room needs no AI key or additional server packages.

CURRENT PILOT
The app runs only on this PC and includes one original sample paper.
Invite links can be tested in another browser on this PC. Colleagues on other
computers will need a hosted version.

Your personal math reader and the discussion design demo remain separate options.
See README.md for advanced setup and development details.
