COMPANION — START HERE

1. Double-click Start Companion.cmd in the Companion folder.
   In the parent outputs folder, Open Companion.cmd opens the same app.
   The launcher starts the installed local math and speech services.

2. Choose a source in the reading map, or open a PDF.

3. Click the microphone or type a request.
   Try: Explain this. Plot x^2. Save note: my observation.
   Use Stop to end the current request.
